Why Shockwave Works

Most treatments manage symptoms. Extracorporeal shockwave therapy (ESWT) targets the root cause, using acoustic pressure waves to stimulate your body's natural healing response in tissue that has stopped progressing on its own.

Rather than masking pain, our rehab-focused plan addresses the underlying mechanics, helping you build a foundation for lasting recovery and a safe return to activity.

Focused vs. Radial Shockwave

Shockwave devices come in two types, and the difference is in how deep the energy reaches.

Focused shockwave, or focused ESWT (fESWT), generates acoustic energy that converges at a set depth below the skin, so the pressure wave is concentrated on the specific tissue being treated. Radial shockwave generates its energy at the applicator head and disperses it outward as it travels, so the effect is strongest close to the surface and spreads more broadly.

Both our Greeley and Denver clinics use focused shockwave devices. Many clinics that advertise shockwave therapy use radial devices, which work differently. If a deeper structure is involved, such as an Achilles tendon or a hip, it is worth asking any provider which type they actually use.

What Treatment Looks Like

  1. Evaluate the condition and movement pattern. We assess where the pain is coming from and what's keeping it overloaded.
  2. Deliver focused shockwave treatment. The treatment is used to stimulate healing in tissue that has stalled out.
  3. Build strength and tolerance between visits. You get the rehab side too, so the area is better prepared for real-life loading.
  4. Adjust based on your response. The plan isn't one-size-fits-all; it evolves as pain, function, and activity tolerance improve.
