### Worth knowing about the review that got them here

Codex reviewed the articles four times and found real problems each time,
including two safety defects I'd have shipped:

- **Cauda equina syndrome was under-triaged.** I had bladder/bowel dysfunction
  and saddle numbness under "see a physician promptly." That's a surgical
  emergency where delay causes permanent loss of function. Now an explicit
  "go to an emergency department now" block.
- **No emergency guidance after discussing stroke risk.** The article explained
  cervical artery dissection and then said to discuss it with your provider.
  Now an explicit 911 block with the CDC stroke warning signs.

**And one I want to flag plainly: I fabricated a citation.** I cited an NIH/NINDS
page for cauda equina whose URL I constructed by pattern rather than from a real
search result. It doesn't exist. A MedlinePlus URL I then tried as a replacement
turned out to be about a completely unrelated condition. Both looked entirely
plausible. It's now replaced with a StatPearls chapter I actually fetched and
confirmed, and there's a test pinning every citation to URLs that were verified
to resolve and be on-topic. Worth knowing that this failure mode is real even
when everything else is carefully sourced — check the links yourself.
